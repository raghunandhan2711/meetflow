A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

Before your
next meeting.

How do I get started?+

Open the workspace and choose New meeting. Upload a recording, record your microphone, or paste a transcript. You can also explore a sample before adding your own meeting.

Does MeetFlow join my calls?+

No. This version processes recordings after you submit them. It does not join Zoom or Google Meet calls, or transcribe a meeting live.

Where does my meeting data go?+

Recordings and meeting history are stored in your local workspace. Transcription runs locally with the current configuration. Transcript text is sent to Groq for AI analysis; reviewed task details are sent to Jira when you choose to send them.

Can I check and edit the AI results?+

Yes. Check source quotes and the transcript, edit task details, correct speaker names, and add your own tasks. AI can make mistakes, so review the results before acting on them.

Is this ready for my whole company?+

This edition is a single-user college project running locally. It demonstrates the complete meeting-to-action workflow. Shared accounts and public hosting are not included.
